Manual

4 · Mezo integration

The account contract is the borrower.

  • BorrowerOperations: openTrove, repayMUSD, closeTrove, addColl, withdrawColl, claimCollateral
  • TroveManager.getEntireDebtAndColl: live debt including accrued interest
  • Respects the 1,800 MUSD minimum net debt and 200 MUSD gas compensation; caps repayment in-block
  • MUSD EIP-2612 permit for one-transaction customer payments
  • Liquidation, redemption, surplus claim and recovery mode handled
5 · Security & architecture

Keepers can repay. Nothing else.

Keeper can

Call executeRepayment: repay this account's own Trove from pending income, within policy and Mezo's floor. Zero fee.

Keeper cannot

Borrow, withdraw collateral, touch operating cash, change owner/policy, pay anyone, or call arbitrary contracts.

Clones initialized atomically by an admin-less factory · nonReentrant everywhere · 61 Foundry tests incl. fuzz & invariants · fork test on real Mezo bytecode · Slither triaged · Sourcify exact match. Self-review, not an audit.

7 · Business hypothesis & roadmap

Tie repayment to revenue, and debt falls on its own.

Hypothesis (unvalidated): businesses with recurring MUSD revenue will keep lower loan-to-value if repayment is automatic. Possible model: a small, capped share of repaid amounts.
