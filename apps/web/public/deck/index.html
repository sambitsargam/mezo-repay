4 · Mezo integration

The account contract is the borrower.

  • BorrowerOperations: openTrove, repayMUSD, closeTrove, addColl, withdrawColl, claimCollateral (close, addColl, withdrawColl run live)
  • TroveManager.getEntireDebtAndColl: live debt including accrued interest
  • Respects the 1,800 MUSD minimum net debt and 200 MUSD gas compensation; caps repayment in-block
  • MUSD EIP-2612 permit for one-transaction customer payments
  • Liquidation, redemption, surplus claim and recovery mode handled
5 · Loan safety & security

Guard the loan, close it from income, and nothing else.

Collateral guard

Below the owner's trigger ratio, anyone can call protect: pending MUSD repays debt first, then BTC from the owner's buffer goes into the same Trove via addColl. Live: 158.2% → 177.4%.

Auto-close (new in v3)

Partial repayment stops at Mezo's minimum debt. With the owner's opt-in, income keeps saving, and once pending covers the payoff the keeper calls executeClose. Live: 1,801.80 MUSD burned, 0 from operating cash.

Keeper cannot

Borrow, withdraw collateral, touch operating cash, change owner, policy or guard, pay itself, or send funds anywhere but the account's own Trove.

7 · Revenue model & roadmap

The business pays a small, capped fee only when the loan gets safer or smaller.

Repayment fee

0.25% of each automated repayment, capped at 5 MUSD. 150 MUSD repaid → 0.375 MUSD.

Guard fee

0.5% of value moved by protect, capped at 10 MUSD. Auto-close: flat 5 MUSD per loan closed.

Who pays

The account owner (the business). Payers pay nothing. Testnet fee is zero; fees are planned, not deployed.

Why: 10,000 MUSD/month revenue at 30% → ≈7.50 MUSD/month in fees to keep LTV falling and a guard watching the ratio. Next: real pilot users, redundant keepers, invoice exports, audit before mainnet.
